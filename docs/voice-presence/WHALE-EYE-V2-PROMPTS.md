# Whale Eye V2 — geração dos assets

Modo de geração: ImageGen integrado, edição por referências raster. Os originais gerados foram mantidos no diretório de imagens do Codex e copiados para `public/nexo/`.

## Asset aberto

Referências: as três imagens fornecidas pelo usuário e a primeira iteração do olho orgânico para alinhamento do componente.

```text
Use case: style-transfer and precise asset redesign.
Asset type: final open-state, full-bleed square raster layer for the Nexo Living Eye voice interface.
Reference priority: Images 1, 2 and 3 define the exact living visual language to preserve: a massive whale-like eye, heavy asymmetric cetacean eyelids, wet dark skin, a deep almost-black blue globe, a restrained luminous cyan inner rim, and especially the delicate organic branching structures floating visibly inside the eye. Image 4 provides prior UI alignment and surrounding skin scale only.
Primary request: create a more alive version that unmistakably follows the references. The eye must feel conscious and internally active: glassy depth, subtle underwater caustic reflections, tiny wet specular highlights, faint suspended particles, minute irregular color variation and a clear organic branching network inside the dark central globe. The branches resemble submerged nerves or bioluminescent roots—fine, asymmetric, naturally fading, concentrated in the upper-middle interior, never a decorative radial iris.
Subject: one animal eye recessed into a huge dark whale-like face. Heavy unequal upper and lower folds compress the globe. The visible globe remains dark navy/near-black, with blue light coming from depth rather than a painted bright iris. The center is bottomless, subtly irregular and animal; no clean human pupil.
Composition: 1:1 square, full bleed, no card. Eye centered near 50% horizontal and 51% vertical for UI overlays. Eye opening roughly 40–48% of frame width, large enough to reveal the internal living structures while the organic whale skin still dominates. Preserve the reference's cinematic close-up presence.
Materials: scarred cetacean skin, dense pores and grooves, wet membranes, slight mucus-like gloss at the lid edge, glassy ocular surface.
Lighting: dark abyssal environment, directional blue-black grazing light on the skin, cyan restricted to inner ocular depth and wet reflections.
Constraints: photoreal; asymmetric; alive; exact square framing; no text; no logo; no UI controls; no background frame; no rounded corners.
Avoid: human radial iris spokes, bright flat electric-blue iris, perfectly circular neon ring, clean circular pupil, white human sclera, eyelashes, eyebrows, makeup, camera lens, robot eye, generic AI orb, symmetry, plastic skin, over-sharpened CGI.
```

## Asset fechado

Referências: asset aberto final e estado fechado anterior apenas como indicação estrutural.

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit.
Asset type: matching fully closed-state raster layer for the Nexo Living Eye animation.
Input images: Image 1 is authoritative. Preserve its exact creature identity, full-bleed square framing, whale skin, pores, grooves, droplets, scars, lighting, color, scale, asymmetry and eye position. Image 2 is only a structural example that the eye must be fully closed.
Primary request: make Image 1 blink fully closed. Replace only the visible globe and open ocular gap with two heavy overlapping asymmetric cetacean eyelids meeting in one moist, irregular, slightly curved horizontal seam at the same location. Surrounding folds should compress naturally as a next animation frame of the exact same living animal.
Appearance: photoreal dark whale skin, wet lid margins, subtle specular moisture along the seam, dense organic texture and quiet underwater darkness. Preserve the dominant upper fold and the lower skin mass from Image 1.
Constraints: 1:1 square, full bleed, same crop and lighting as Image 1; no visible iris, globe, blue glow, pupil or sclera; no text, logo, UI, frame, border or rounded card.
Avoid: human eyelids, eyelashes, eyebrow, makeup, symmetrical folds, smooth plastic skin, stitched wound, camera lens, robot eye, AI orb.
```
